# Rive asset credit

`rocket.riv` is the example asset bundled in the `rive-flutter` runtime
repository: https://github.com/rive-app/rive-flutter (path:
`example/assets/rocket.riv`), used to demonstrate the `rive` package.

License: MIT (the whole `rive-flutter` repository, including its example
assets, is MIT-licensed — see the repository's `LICENSE` file). Copyright (c)
2020 Rive.

Used in-app on the session summary screen when the learner finishes with a
very high score (>= 95%), as the one moment in the app that gets a fully
interactive animation rather than a static illustration.
